Game Rules RTP 94.00%

HOW TO PLAY

Set your bet with the − and + buttons, then tap SPIN or swipe up on the reels. Wins pay left to right on 25 fixed lines, highest win per line only. Line wins are multiplied by the line bet (total bet ÷ 25).

FREE SPINS

3, 4 or 5 SCATTER symbols anywhere award 10, 15 or 20 Free Spins. All wins during Free Spins are doubled. 3 or more SCATTERs during the feature award 5 extra spins.

SUPER WHEEL

3 BONUS symbols on reels 1, 3 and 5 start the Super Wheel. Spin once to win a credit prize of 10× to 50× total bet, Free Spins, or one of the four jackpots.

JACKPOTS

GRAND, MAJOR, MINOR and MINI are progressive and shared across Jack Vault. A small part of every bet feeds them. They are won only from the Super Wheel and are paid in full regardless of bet.

MALFUNCTION

Malfunction voids all pays and plays. An interrupted round resumes from the same state when you return.
